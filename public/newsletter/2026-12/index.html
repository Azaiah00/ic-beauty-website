Don’t forget

Your hair needs moisture,
not just oil.

As the air gets drier, your strands may need more attention to prevent dryness and breakage.

Don’t neglect shampooing and conditioning between salon visits!

Hydrates

Cleanse + condition

A consistent cleansing and conditioning routine helps maintain moisture.

Certain oils

Oil alone doesn’t hydrate your hair, but certain oils can help reduce moisture loss.
