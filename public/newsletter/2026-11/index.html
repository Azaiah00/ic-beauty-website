Wellness cabinet spotlight

Vitamin D3

 

You may want to add this if you…

Spend most of your time indoors

Get limited sunshine

Have had low vitamin D levels before

Why it matters for us: melanin reduces how much vitamin D our skin makes from sunlight. Add aging, indoor lifestyles and shorter winter days, and keeping healthy levels gets harder, especially this time of year.

Vitamin D supports bone health, muscle function, immune health and normal hair follicle growth.
